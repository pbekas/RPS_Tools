Bottom line: yes — but schedule matters more than headcount alone
You are missing about 1 in 8 inbound callers overall. The problem is not uniform: it spikes at 4–5pm PT, on Fridays, and on extensions that ring with almost no answers. Peak bursts need ~9 concurrent agents in a 15-minute window — long calls (median 2m, avg 4m) tie people up during morning and mid-afternoon rushes.
